# FreshBooks — Freelancer Accounting Onboarding | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

FreshBooks (Canada, 30M+ users, acquired by Renaissance) is the **freelancer/self-employed accounting alternative to QBO.** Unlike QBO (double-entry accounting, powerful but complex), FreshBooks is single-entry and simpler: "accounting for people who don't do accounting." Onboarding: business name, type, currency, tax name/rate, invoice customization (logo, colors, payment terms), bank/credit card connection, expense categories (simpler than QBO chart of accounts), client import, project/time tracking setup.

**Key insight:** FreshBooks wins on simplicity — freelancers who find QBO overwhelming choose FreshBooks. The simpler onboarding (no double-entry, no chart of accounts complexity, no reconciliation training needed) converts users QBO loses. QBO should offer a "simple mode" for freelancers.
