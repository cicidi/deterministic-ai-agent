# Deutsche Bank — German Corporate Banking | **Rating:** 2.3/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05
Deutsche Bank (Germany, 20M+ customers, $1.5T+ assets) represents the most complex corporate/ investment bank KYC in the research. Post-financial-crisis compliance overhaul made KYC notoriously slow. Individual banking: Postbank subsidiary (branch-based, PostIdent KYC). Deutsche Bank Blue (digital banking app, launched 2022).
