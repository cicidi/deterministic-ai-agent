## Executive Summary — Why Chime is #8

Chime's "second-chance banking" model proves that **rejecting traditional risk signals opens the largest addressable market.** Here's what that means concretely:

**What Chime rejects:** ChexSystems (banking history blacklist), credit checks (FICO), minimum balances, overdraft fees, monthly fees. A person who bounced a check 3 years ago and got blacklisted by every bank can open a Chime account in 2 minutes.

**What Chime uses instead:** SSN-based identity verification (Socure/LexisNexis), Plaid bank transaction data (income patterns, spending patterns, average balance), direct deposit history. When a user establishes regular direct deposits, Chime progressively unlocks features — SpotMe overdraft ($20→$200), MyPay wage advance (up to $500), higher APY tiers.

**QBO equivalent:** A small business with a thin credit file, bad personal credit, or no business credit history gets rejected by traditional banks for a business account or credit. QBO could offer "second-chance accounting" — approve based on QBO transaction data (consistent revenue, low dispute rate, good cash flow), not FICO or traditional business credit reports. Feature unlocks based on QBO usage behavior, not external credit scores.

**Concrete numbers:** 2-minute mobile-only signup. 4.8★ App Store (750K+ reviews). Partner bank model (Bancorp/Stride). SpotMe $20-200 based on DD history. MyPay up to $500. Tiered APY (0.75% → 3.75%) based on qualifying direct deposits.
