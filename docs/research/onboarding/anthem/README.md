## Executive Summary
Anthem (now Elevance Health, 47M+ members, largest BCBS affiliate in 14 states) operates under the Blue Cross Blue Shield brand — the most recognized health insurance brand in the US. UNIQUE: BCBS network portability — members can access BCBS providers nationwide through the BlueCard program. Anthem's acquisition of Beacon Health Options (behavioral health) and IngenioRx (PBM) mirrors Cigna's integrated model. Sydney Health app is the digital front-end.
