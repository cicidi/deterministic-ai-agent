# Afterpay — BNPL Onboarding (Block Inc.) | **Rating:** 4.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Afterpay (Block Inc/Square acquired 2022 for $29B) is the **original BNPL leader** — pay in 4 installments, 0% interest, no credit check (soft check only). Integrated into Square's ecosystem post-acquisition. Onboarding at checkout: name, DOB, last 4 SSN, mobile, email. Soft credit check only. Late fees capped (and banned entirely in some markets). Afterpay Card (virtual + physical). Afterpay Money (bank account in AU). Square integration: Afterpay available to Square merchants natively.

**Key insight:** Afterpay's Square integration creates the only BNPL + POS hardware ecosystem — a merchant can offer Afterpay at point of sale with Square hardware. QBO: embedded lending at point of accounting need.
