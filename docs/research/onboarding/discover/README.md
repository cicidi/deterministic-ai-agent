# Discover — Credit Card Network + Online Bank | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Discover (3rd largest US credit card network) is **both a network AND an issuer** — like Amex, a closed loop. Discover Cashback Debit (1% cash back on debit purchases — unique in US banking). No monthly fees, no minimums. Free FICO score for all (not just customers). 24/7 US-based customer service. Onboarding: SSN, ID, DOB, address, phone, employment. Digital account opening. Discover it credit cards (5% rotating categories, cashback match first year). Discover Loans (personal, student, home equity). Discover was the first major bank to eliminate most fees (late payment, overlimit, returned payment) on all cards.

**Key insight:** 1% debit cash back is the most unique checking account feature among major US banks — it pays you to use your own money. Combined with "no fees on anything" positioning, Discover is the most consumer-friendly traditional bank. QBO: "earn rewards for completing bookkeeping tasks" — gamification of compliance.
