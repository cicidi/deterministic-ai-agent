# Truist — Merger-Driven Digital Banking Onboarding
> **Industry:** Banking | **Rating:** 3.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
Truist (7th largest US bank, BB&T+SunTrust merger 2019) is the best case study for "merger as digital transformation catalyst." Had to build unified onboarding from two legacy stacks — resulting in relatively modern digital flow (end-to-end online, not just lead-gen). Truist One Checking ($50 min, $12/mo waivable) has 5-level auto-tiering. Confidence Account ($5/mo, no-overdraft) for underbanked. Lighthouse AI financial wellness tools. Early overdraft fee elimination (2022).

**Key insight:** Clean-slate merger rebuild produced a digital onboarding experience closer to fintech than traditional banks — proving that legacy banks CAN modernize when forced to rebuild from scratch.

**Rating:** 3.0 — good digital UX by traditional bank standards but still heavy KYC.
