# Gemini — Regulated US Crypto Exchange | **Rating:** 3.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Gemini (Winklevoss twins, NYDFS-regulated) is the **most regulated US crypto exchange** — Trust Company charter under NY Banking Law. Security-first, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001. Onboarding: SSN, photo ID + selfie (Jumio/Onfido), proof of address, source of funds (for higher limits), bank account verification. Gemini Earn (discontinued after Genesis bankruptcy — a cautionary tale on crypto lending). Gemini Credit Card (waitlist). Gemini Dollar (GUSD, regulated stablecoin). Gemini Custody, Gemini Clearing, Gemini Pay. ActiveTrader (professional trading interface with lower fees than basic).

**Key insight:** Gemini's NYDFS trust charter means they're regulated like a bank — the strongest regulatory compliance in crypto. Bigger compliance burden = slower onboarding, but institutional clients prefer it. QBO: compliance as competitive differentiator.
