## Executive Summary — Why Brex is #9

Brex proved that **company financial data can replace personal credit for SMB underwriting.** This is the single most relevant lesson for QBO.

**The traditional corporate card:** Founder applies with personal SSN, personal credit check, personal guarantee. If the founder has bad credit, the company gets rejected — even if the company has $2M in the bank and $500K in revenue. The founder's personal finances determine the company's credit access.

**Brex's model:** Founder links the company's business bank account via Plaid. Brex sees the bank balance, revenue patterns, burn rate, investor backing. Credit limit = f(company bank balance), NOT f(founder FICO). No personal SSN. No personal guarantee. No personal credit check. 10-minute application. Instant virtual card.

**QBO's opportunity is MASSIVE here:** QBO already has richer company financial data than Plaid can see — years of transaction history, categorized income/expenses, profit margins, cash flow trends, tax data (via TurboTax integration), payroll data, invoice payment history. QBO knows more about a business's financial health than any bank. Yet QBO doesn't use this data for credit/payroll/lending decisions. Intuit owns Credit Karma (130M credit profiles) + TurboTax (50M tax returns) + QBO (income/expense data). The combined dataset could underwrite SMB lending better than any bank.

**Concrete numbers:** 10-minute application. Instant virtual card. Credit limit scales with bank balance. $0 personal guarantee. 2M+ cards issued. Daily repayment (not monthly). Brex Cash (business bank account). Brex Empower (spend management platform for enterprises).

## Detailed Comparison: QBO vs Brex

### The Underwriting Revolution
| Decision Factor | Traditional Corporate Card (Amex, Chase) | Brex | QBO's Future With Brex Model |
|----------------|----------------------------------------|------|------------------------------|
| **Founder's FICO** | Required. Below 680 = denied | Not checked. Not collected | Not needed. QBO transaction data is a better signal |
| **Founder's personal guarantee** | Required. Founder personally liable | Not required. Company liable | Not needed for QBO Capital/credit offers |
| **Company bank balance** | May be requested as supporting doc | Plaid instant verification. Primary underwriting factor. $50K balance = ~$15K credit limit | QBO already has this data. Instant. More accurate than a one-time balance snapshot |
| **Company revenue** | Tax returns or bank statements | Plaid transaction data. Revenue patterns, growth rate, consistency | QBO has categorized income, revenue trends, seasonal patterns, YoY growth |
| **Investor backing** | Not considered | Strong positive signal. YC/Sequoia/a16z backing = higher credit limit | QBO Knows payroll size, hiring velocity, burn rate — strong viability signals |
| **Time to decision** | Days to weeks | 10 minutes | Should be instant |
