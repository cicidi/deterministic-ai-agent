# Affirm — BNPL with Credit Decision Onboarding | **Rating:** 4.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Affirm (17M+ users, public AFRM) is the **transparent BNPL** — no late fees, no compound interest, clear total cost before purchase. Onboarding: name, DOB, last 4 SSN, phone — soft credit check at checkout. Affirm Money (savings), Affirm Card (debit+). Unlike Klarna (email+ZIP), Affirm does a soft credit pull affecting the offered terms. Merchants: Shopify, Amazon, Walmart, Target integration. Split Pay (4 payments, 0% APR), monthly installments (0-36% APR, clearly shown). Affirm's underwriting: proprietary risk model using alternative data beyond traditional credit.

**Key insight:** Affirm's "no gotchas" transparency (exact dollar amount, no late fees, no compounding) inverts the traditional lending model. The upfront disclosure IS the conversion tool — showing "you pay $0 in interest" for 0% APR offers.
