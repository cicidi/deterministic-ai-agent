# MetLife — Group Benefits + Life Insurance Onboarding
> **Industry:** Insurance / Group Benefits + Life | **Rating:** 3.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
MetLife (largest US life insurer, 90M+ customers globally) is the **group benefits giant** — employer-sponsored life, dental, disability, vision. Onboarding is fundamentally different from auto/home: employer selects plans → employee self-enrolls during benefits enrollment → MetLife processes enrollment data from employer's HR system. Individual life insurance follows traditional agent + medical exam model.

| Dimension | Rating | Key Pattern |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Field Minimization | 3 | Group: employer census file pre-fills everything. Individual life: medical exam + blood work + full health questionnaire |
| Risk Intelligence | 5 | Life underwriting: medical exam, blood work, MIB, MVR (actuarial life expectancy models). 150+ years of mortality data |
| Conversion | 3 | Group: auto-enrollment + benefits fair. Individual: agent-driven, high-touch, medical exam barrier |
| Agentic/AI | 2 | Limited. Group benefits enrollment portal. No chatbot. Traditional insurer |
| Entitlement | 3 | Group benefits: coverage ladder. Individual: term, whole, universal life. Annuity products |

**Key insight:** Life insurance onboarding is the most invasive in insurance — medical exam, blood draw, full health history. MetLife's group channel (zero-touch employer census) vs. individual channel (maximum-touch agent + exam) is the widest friction gap in any insurance category. QBO lesson: group/employer channel = near-zero onboarding friction through employer pre-fill.

**QBO takeaway:** Employer-subsidized QBO (like group benefits) with employer census pre-fill would achieve near-zero onboarding friction for employees.
