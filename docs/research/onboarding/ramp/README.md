## Executive Summary
Ramp (Brex competitor, $7.6B+ valuation) focused on **"spend management, not lending"** — corporate cards with automated expense policies, receipt matching, and savings insights. Onboarding: EIN + business bank (Plaid) + formation docs + employee list. No personal guarantee. No credit check for founders. AI-powered receipt matching (OCR + ML) and "Ramp Intelligence" that finds SaaS spend optimization, duplicate subscriptions, and contract negotiation opportunities.
