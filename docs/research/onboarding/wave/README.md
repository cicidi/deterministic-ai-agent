# Wave — Free Accounting Onboarding | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Wave (acquired by H&R Block 2019) is the **free accounting alternative** — accounting, invoicing, receipt scanning all free. Revenue from payments (credit card processing) and payroll. Onboarding: business name/type, currency, bank/credit card connection (Plaid), income/expense category auto-suggestion, invoice customization, tax calculation setup. The "free" positioning changes the onboarding conversion calculus — no pricing friction at all.

**Key insight:** Wave's "free forever" eliminates the single biggest onboarding drop-off: pricing. Users who would never pay for QBO start with Wave. QBO should offer a genuinely free tier (not just trial) that captures users who will eventually upgrade.
