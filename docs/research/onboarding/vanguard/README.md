# Vanguard — Low-Cost Investment Onboarding | **Rating:** 3.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Vanguard ($8T+ AUM, 50M+ investors) is the **index fund giant** — invented the index fund (1976, Jack Bogle). Client-owned mutual structure (fund shareholders own Vanguard, not external investors). Onboarding: SSN, ID, DOB, address, employment, income, net worth, investment experience, risk tolerance (FINRA suitability). Known for: lowest expenses (0.08% avg expense ratio), $0 commission trading, Vanguard Personal Advisor Services (hybrid robo-advisor + human, 0.30% fee, $50K min). Vanguard Digital Advisor (pure robo, $3K min).

**Key insight:** Vanguard's "client-owned" structure means lower fees but also lower investment in UX. Their onboarding is functional but clunky compared to Robinhood/Schwab/Fidelity. Proves that brand trust + lowest fees can compensate for mediocre onboarding — but for how long?
