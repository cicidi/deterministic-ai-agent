# Nationwide — Auto + Home + Farm + Commercial Onboarding
> **Industry:** Insurance / Auto + Home + Farm + Commercial | **Rating:** 3.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
Nationwide (8th largest US P&C insurer, Fortune 100) has the **most diverse product portfolio** among insurers studied — auto, home, farm/ranch, commercial, pet, motorcycle, boat, RV. SmartRide telematics, SmartMiles pay-per-mile. "On Your Side" claims promise. Independent + exclusive agent network.

**Key insight:** Nationwide's farm/ranch insurance is a unique vertical — onboarding requires specialized property details (acreage, livestock, equipment, outbuildings) that no other major insurer captures digitally. Represents the vertical-specific onboarding challenge that generalist insurers struggle with.

**QBO takeaway:** Vertical-specific onboarding paths. Construction needs job costing forms. Restaurant needs inventory. Professional services needs time tracking. Learn from Nationwide's farm/ranch specialization.
