# Kaiser Permanente — Integrated Care Model Onboarding
> **Industry:** Healthcare / Integrated Delivery System | **Rating:** 3.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
Kaiser Permanente (12.5M members, operates in 8 states + DC) is the largest **integrated delivery system** in the US — Kaiser is simultaneously the insurer (Kaiser Foundation Health Plan), the hospital system (Kaiser Foundation Hospitals), and the physician group (Permanente Medical Groups). This creates a unique onboarding: one enrollment activates insurance + assigns a PCP + gives access to Kaiser's closed-network facilities. Unlike every other insurer studied, Kaiser doesn't just pay for care — it delivers care. This makes onboarding literally "sign up, get a doctor."

| Dimension | Rating | Key Pattern |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Field Minimization | 4 | Employer auto-enrollment dominates (80%+ of members). Individual: ACA marketplace + medical history questionnaire |
| Risk Intelligence | 3 | MIB + integrated EHR (Epic/HealthConnect) + claims data. All care delivered in-network = more data |
| Conversion | 4 | "One signup = doctor + hospital + pharmacy" — strongest integrated value proposition in healthcare |
| Agentic/AI | 4 | kp.org portal, AI-powered symptom checker, video visits, automated Rx refill, HealthConnect EHR |
| Entitlement | 4 | Closed-network model = all Kaiser facilities accessible immediately. No out-of-network complexity |

**Key insight:** Kaiser's closed-loop model (insurer = hospital = doctor group) means onboarding is literally "sign up, get a doctor, start care" — the most integrated healthcare onboarding in the US. QBO equivalent: "Sign up, get a dashboard, get an accountant" — integrated service delivery, not just software.

**QBO takeaway:** Kaiser proves that vertical integration (software + services + network) creates the strongest onboarding value proposition. QBO should bundle: software + accountant matching + tax filing in one signup.
