# Upgrade — Credit-First Fintech Onboarding | **Rating:** 3.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Upgrade (founded by former LendingClub CEO Renaud Laplanche) combines **credit monitoring + lending + banking.** Upgrade Card (hybrid installment loan + Visa card), personal loans ($1K-$50K), Rewards Checking (2% cash back on debit), performance savings. Onboarding: SSN, ID, DOB, address, income, employment — soft credit pull to show offers. Credit Health (free credit monitoring for all). Upgrade's unique model: card purchases automatically convert to fixed-rate installment plans.

**Key insight:** Credit Health as top-of-funnel — free credit monitoring for non-customers, converting to card/loan applications when score drops (refinancing opportunity) or score rises (home purchase). QBO: "Business Health Score" as top-of-funnel with product recommendations based on financial signals.
