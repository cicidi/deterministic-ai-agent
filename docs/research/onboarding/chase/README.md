# Chase (JPMorgan Chase) — Traditional Banking Onboarding Analysis

> **Industry:** Banking / Chartered National Bank | **Type:** Consumer + Business KYC/KYB | **Rating:** 3.2/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary

Chase (largest US bank, $4T+ assets, 80M+ customers, 4,700+ branches) has the **strongest fraud consortium data** in US banking — as an Early Warning Services (EWS) co-owner, Chase has privileged access to real-time fraud data that fintech competitors cannot match. Digital onboarding is solid (55M+ mobile users, full account opening in-app), but the model remains heavy KYC upfront. The key innovations: Credit Journey as no-obligation top-of-funnel (free credit score available to non-customers), Overdraft Assist ($50 buffer, no fee), and First Republic white-glove service integration for HNW clients.

**One-sentence:** Chase's EWS co-ownership gives it the best fraud data in US banking — a moat fintechs cannot match — but its KYC remains as heavy as BOA's.

**Key QBO Insights:**
1. **Credit Journey = QBO free-tier funnel** — offer free business health score with no QBO account required. Convert to full product.

3. **New account bonuses** — $300 for checking with DD. QBO: first month free, $200 credit for completing setup.
