# Wells Fargo — Traditional Banking Onboarding

> **Industry:** Banking | **Rating:** 3.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
WF (3rd largest US bank, 70M+ customers) rebuilt onboarding after the 2016 fake accounts scandal ($6B+ penalties). Key changes: explicit consent checkpoints, centralized account opening, eliminated branch quotas. WF co-owns EWS with 6 other major banks giving strong fraud consortium data. $25 min deposit across all accounts. Clear Access Banking ($5/mo, free for 13-24) as acquisition funnel for young customers.

**Key insight:** Post-scandal transparency = forced onboarding discipline. Every product-add requires explicit consent. Overdraft requires Reg E double opt-in. QBO lesson: consent architecture as compliance safeguard.

**Rating:** 3.0 — heavy KYC upfront like all traditional banks, but post-scandal consent framework is the strongest among US banks.
