# Brigit — Financial Health App | **Rating:** 3.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Brigit (4M+ users) combines **cash advances + credit building + budgeting + identity theft protection** — "financial health" positioning vs Dave/EarnIn's "cash advance" positioning. Onboarding: SSN, DOB, address, bank account connection (Plaid). Instant Cash ($250 max advance, $9.99/mo Plus or $14.99/mo Premium). Credit Builder ($20/mo credit builder loan). Finance Helper (bill negotiation, subscription tracking). Earn & Save (side hustle marketplace). Budgeting + spending analytics. Identity theft protection ($1M coverage, Premium plan).

**Key insight:** Brigit bundles multiple financial health services (cash advance + credit builder + ID theft + budget) under one subscription — the "financial wellness platform" approach. Higher ARPU ($10-15/mo vs $1-5 for Chime/Dave). QBO: bundle payroll + tax + banking under one subscription.
