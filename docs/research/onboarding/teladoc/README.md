# Teladoc Health — Telehealth Platform Onboarding | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Teladoc (largest US telehealth provider, acquired Livongo 2020 for $18.5B) is the **"front door" to virtual healthcare.** Dual onboarding: Patient side — employer-sponsored enrollment (auto-enrolled or self-register with insurance info), create Teladoc account, complete medical history questionnaire, link pharmacy preference. Provider side — NPI, medical license, board certification, DEA registration, malpractice insurance verification, state license per telemedicine jurisdiction. Livongo integration: chronic condition management (diabetes, hypertension) with connected devices.

**Key insight:** Teladoc's dual onboarding (patient + provider) mirrors marketplace models. Patients onboard with insurance ID; providers onboard with medical credentials. QBO: dual onboarding — business + accountant, each with different verification requirements.
