# Marcus by Goldman Sachs — Digital Consumer Banking | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Goldman Sachs' consumer banking experiment. High-yield savings (competitive APY), no-fee personal loans, Marcus Invest (robo-advisor), GM credit card (Apple Card partnership). Onboarding: SSN, ID, DOB, address, employment, income — entirely digital. No branches (GS is an investment bank). Apple Card partnership (2019): 12M+ users, Apple's onboarding UX + Goldman's underwriting. GM card partnership. Post-2023 retreat from consumer (sold GreenSky, Marcus Invest to Betterment).

**Key insight:** Goldman proved "Wall Street can do consumer fintech" — then retreated when it wasn't profitable enough. The Apple Card partnership showed the power of tech-company UX + bank underwriting. QBO lesson: partner with tech platforms for onboarding UX.
