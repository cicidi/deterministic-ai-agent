# Robinhood — SWOT vs Intuit QuickBooks Online

## Robinhood SWOT

### Strengths
- **11-field, single-field-per-screen** — industry UX benchmark for consumer finance
- **Try-before-you-buy sandbox** — demonstrates value before asking for data
- **Deferred bank linking** — removes biggest drop-off from signup
- **$1K instant deposit** — immediate trading while ACH settles
- **Cortex AI + Digests** — 95% customer satisfaction for AI stock insights
- **Mobile-first design system** — dark mode #000000, neon green #B7DF2F, 4px grid, Inter font

### Weaknesses
- **$70M FINRA fine** — options approval bot was too aggressive; compliance treated as UX friction
- **Customer support failures** — platform outages locked users out with no recourse
- **Gamification overreach** — confetti, scratch-offs triggered regulatory backlash
- **Crypto UX** — acknowledged as "maze" by Head of Crypto
- **PDT restrictions** — aggressive enforcement hurt active traders
