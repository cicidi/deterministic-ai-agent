# Alipay (Ant Group) — Chinese Superapp Onboarding | **Rating:** 4.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Alipay (1B+ users globally, Ant Group subsidiary) is the **world's largest digital payment platform** — and the original superapp. Onboarding is unique: tiered real-name verification (Level 1-3). Level 1: phone number + bank card (basic payment). Level 2: photo ID + facial recognition (higher limits). Level 3: enhanced KYC (full access). The **Sesame Credit** system (social credit scoring for financial services) is a unique entitlement model — higher score = deposit-free hotel, bike rental, higher payment limits. Yu'e Bao (money market fund integrated into wallet). Ant Fortune (investments). MYbank (online-only bank for SMBs).

**Key insight:** Sesame Credit scoring is the most ambitious entitlement model in global fintech — a single score unlocking benefits across hundreds of services. QBO equivalent: "QBO Business Health Score" affecting credit offers, accountant recommendations, payroll limits.
