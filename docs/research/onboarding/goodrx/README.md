# GoodRx — Prescription Discount Onboarding | **Rating:** 4.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

GoodRx (20M+ monthly users) is the **prescription price comparison + discount platform.** Onboarding: search medication → see prices at nearby pharmacies → get free coupon/discount card → show at pharmacy. ZERO KYC. Name + email only to save favorite pharmacies. GoodRx Gold ($9.99/mo) for deeper discounts. GoodRx Care (telehealth). Provider Mode for HCPs. GoodRx's innovation: price transparency in a healthcare market where nobody knows what anything costs.

**Key insight:** Zero-KYC healthcare — the rarest thing in the research. GoodRx proves that not all healthcare requires PHI/PII collection. "Search for your medication" is the lowest-friction healthcare entry point studied. QBO: "Search for your industry → see accounting template."
