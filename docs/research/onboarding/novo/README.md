# Novo — Freelancer/SMB Banking Onboarding | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Novo (200K+ SMBs) is the **freelancer and small business bank** — competing with Mercury for the "below startup" tier. Free business checking, Novo Reserves (up to 10 sub-accounts for budgeting), Novo Invoicing, Stripe/Square/PayPal integration, QuickBooks/Xero/FreshBooks integration. Middlesex Federal Savings partner bank. Onboarding: EIN/SSN, business registration, personal ID — faster than traditional business accounts (10 min). No minimum balance, no monthly fees.

**Key insight:** Novo's sub-account "Reserves" feature lets freelancers budget like a business — allocate funds to tax, rent, equipment, etc. within one account. QBO integration feeds transaction data directly. The QBO tie-in makes this the most "QBO-adjacent" onboarding in the study.

**Rating:** 3.5 | **QBO takeaway:** Direct QBO integration as onboarding feature. Sub-account budgeting that mirrors accounting categories.
