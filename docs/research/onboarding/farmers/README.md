# Farmers Insurance — Agent-Centric Insurance Onboarding
> **Industry:** Insurance / Auto + Home | **Rating:** 3.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
Farmers (5th largest US auto insurer, owned by Zurich Insurance Group) is the most **agent-centric** of major insurers — 21,000+ exclusive and independent agents, with digital as a supplement not the primary channel. Signal telematics (30-day test drive before committing). Farmers Friendly Review (annual agent check-in). Unlike Geico (direct-only) and Progressive (hybrid with strong digital), Farmers' onboarding flows through agents. This represents the traditional insurance paradigm that fintech disruption targets.

| Dimension | Rating | Key Pattern |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Field Minimization | 2 | Agent-mediated = full data collection upfront. Digital channel is secondary/limited |
| Risk Intelligence | 4 | MVR + CLUE + credit-based score + agent risk assessment. Zurich global underwriting |
| Conversion | 3 | Agent relationship = high conversion on qualified leads. Low on cold digital traffic |
| Agentic/AI | 2 | Signal telematics. Limited self-service AI. Agent-centric model |
| Entitlement | 3 | Coverage ladder. Multi-policy bundling (auto+home+life+business). Signal discount |

**Key insight:** Farmers represents the "agent as onboarding intermediary" model — agent handles SSN/credit sensitivity, explains underwriting decisions, bundles products. QBO equivalent: accountant-assisted QBO setup where accountant guides business through verification.

**QBO takeaway:** Signal's "30-day test drive before committing" is a unique trust pattern. QBO equivalent: "Connect your bank, we'll show you a free financial health report. No payment required. Subscribe only if you find value."
