# Progressive Insurance — Auto Insurance (#10 Overall)

> **Industry:** Insurance / Auto | **Onboarding Type:** Consumer P&C | **Overall:** 4.6/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

---

## Executive Summary — Why Progressive is #10

Progressive's **Name Your Price tool** inverts the insurance quoting paradigm — and it's the single most effective conversion pattern in the insurance industry.

**Traditional insurance quote:** Enter 20 fields (name, DOB, SSN, driver's license, VIN, prior insurance, driving history, vehicle details) → wait → see price → sticker shock → abandon. User invested 10 minutes filling a form to be told a number they can't afford.

**Name Your Price:** "What's your monthly budget for car insurance?" → user enters $80 → Progressive shows: "For $80/mo, you can get: liability 50/100/50, $1,000 deductible, no comprehensive or collision. For $95/mo, you can add comprehensive with $500 deductible." User now sees what they CAN get, not what they can't afford. They can adjust up or down. The budget anchors the conversation, not the sticker price.

**QBO equivalent:** "What's your monthly budget for accounting software?" → user enters $25 → QBO shows "For $25/mo, you can get: bank connections, basic P&L, 5 invoices/mo, mobile receipt capture. For $50/mo, add: payroll for 1 employee, tax-ready reports, accountant access." User chooses based on budget, not sticker shock.

**The Comparison Tool** takes this further: it shows Progressive's rate alongside Geico's, State Farm's, and Allstate's — on Progressive's own website. Radical transparency builds trust. Even when Progressive isn't cheapest, users remember the honesty.

**Concrete numbers:** 3rd largest US auto insurer (~13% market share). Snapshot telematics pioneered in 2008. Average $328 annual savings for safe drivers. 99% of customers earn at least one discount. Flo branding (one of the most recognized ad campaigns in insurance).

## Detailed Comparison: QBO vs Progressive

### Budget-First vs Price-First
| Step | Progressive (Name Your Price) | Traditional Insurance | QBO Current |
|------|------------------------------|----------------------|-------------|
| 1 | "What's your monthly budget?" | "Enter your personal information" (20 fields) | "Choose your plan" (Simple Start $15 / Essentials $30 / Plus $45 / Advanced $100) |
| 2 | Progressive shows what coverage fits $80 | User enters all fields, sees $120 price | User picks plan, THEN enters 25 fields |
| 3 | User adjusts budget or coverage. Ladder is visible | User may abandon due to sticker shock | User enters SSN, EIN, business license. "Is this really worth $30/mo?" |
| 4 | User buys. Digital ID card instant | User may shop competitors | User may abandon mid-form |

### Price Anchoring — The Psychology
| Technique | Progressive | QBO Should Apply |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Budget anchoring** | "What's your budget?" sets the conversation around what the user CAN afford | "What's your budget for accounting?" User sees what features fit their budget |
| **Ladder display** | "$80 gets you X. $95 gets you X+Y. $120 gets you X+Y+Z." All visible, no hidden surprises | "$15/mo: bank feed + basic reports. $30/mo: + invoicing + receipt capture. $50/mo: + payroll for 2 employees" |
| **Competitor transparency** | Shows Geico, State Farm, Allstate rates alongside Progressive's ON Progressive's site | Shows Xero, FreshBooks, Wave pricing alongside QBO's ON Intuit's site. Radical honesty |
| **Discount stacking** | 99% of customers earn ≥1 discount. Each discount makes the user feel smart | "You qualify for: new business discount (10%), accountant referral discount (5%), annual payment discount (10%)" — personalized to the user |

### What QBO Should Actually Do
| Priority | Action | Model From Progressive | Impact |
|----------|--------|----------------------|--------|
| **P0** | "What's your budget?" → show what QBO tier that buys. No plans page first. Budget-first | Name Your Price budget-first quote tool | Eliminates "is this worth it?" abandonment during the form |
| **P1** | Competitor comparison ON QBO's site. Show Xero, FreshBooks, Wave pricing alongside QBO | Progressive Comparison Tool | Radical transparency builds trust. Users stop comparison-shopping externally |
