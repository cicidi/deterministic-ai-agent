# UnitedHealth Group — Health Insurance Onboarding
> **Industry:** Healthcare | **Rating:** 2.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
UNH (largest US health insurer, 50M+ members) represents healthcare onboarding — governed by HIPAA (PHI), using MIB (Medical Information Bureau) + PBM databases instead of ChexSystems/credit checks. Four channels: employer auto-enrollment (85-95% conversion, 0-5 user fields — the gold standard), ACA marketplace (40-60%), Medicare Advantage (50-65%), and direct-to-consumer (15-30% — 25-40 fields, medical questionnaire 20-50 questions). Optum's AI prior authorization (15-25% denial rate) creates trust deficit.

**Key insight:** Healthcare's "gates on outflow" is hostile — digital card issued immediately, but access to care gated behind AI-driven prior auth. QBO lesson: AI must assist/approve, not deny.

**Rating:** 2.8 — employer auto-enrollment is best-in-class zero-friction. Individual onboarding is the worst among all industries studied.
