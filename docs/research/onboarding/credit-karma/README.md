# Credit Karma — Free Credit Monitoring Onboarding | **Rating:** 4.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Credit Karma (130M+ members, acquired by Intuit 2020 for $8.1B) is the **ultimate top-of-funnel for Intuit products** — free credit scores, credit monitoring, identity monitoring, tax filing integration with TurboTax, checking/savings (Credit Karma Money), personal loans marketplace, credit card recommendations, auto insurance comparison. Onboarding: SSN, DOB, address — soft credit pull from TransUnion + Equifax. NO hard pull, NO payment, NO commitment. Credit Karma makes money via targeted financial product recommendations (credit cards, loans) based on credit profile. The onboarding IS the free value delivery — users get their score instantly.

**Key insight:** Credit Karma's "free forever, no credit card required" model = 130M users who WANT to be monitored. This is the largest financial lead-gen funnel in existence. QBO relationship: Credit Karma can feed SMB owners into QuickBooks — Intuit already owns both. The integration is the opportunity.
