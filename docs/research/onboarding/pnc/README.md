# PNC Bank — Virtual Wallet Onboarding
> **Industry:** Banking | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
PNC (6th largest US bank) created the most innovative product UX among traditional banks: **Virtual Wallet** bundles Spend (checking) + Reserve (short-term) + Growth (savings) into one onboarding flow with visual money management tools (Money Bar, Calendar, Danger Day alerts, Savings Goals/Rules). Low Cash Mode provides intelligent overdraft avoidance. 2021 BBVA USA acquisition expanded to national footprint. $7/mo waived with $500+ DD.

**Key insight:** Bundled 3-account onboarding creates instant sticky relationship. Visual money tools = continuous engagement, not one-time account opening.

**Rating:** 3.5 — best traditional bank product UX but still heavy KYC upfront.
