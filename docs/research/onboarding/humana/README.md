# Humana — Medicare-First Health Insurance Onboarding
> **Industry:** Healthcare / Medicare Advantage | **Rating:** 3.2/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
Humana (17M+ members, #2 Medicare Advantage insurer) is uniquely **Medicare-first** — 85%+ of revenue from Medicare, unlike UNH/Cigna/Anthem which are employer-first. This creates a very different onboarding: 65+ demographic, CMS-regulated enrollment periods (Oct 15-Dec 7 AEP), agent-assisted phone/in-person enrollment, Star Ratings as primary conversion driver. Humana's CenterWell senior-focused primary care clinics and pharmacy (CenterWell Pharmacy) create integrated care delivery.

| Dimension | Rating | Key Pattern |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Field Minimization | 2 | Medicare requires MBI, SSN, Part A/B verification. 65+ demographic = lower digital literacy |
| Risk Intelligence | 4 | CMS risk adjustment (HCC coding), MIB, PBM checks. Medicare-specific frailty/risk scoring |
| Conversion | 4 | CMS Star Ratings = primary conversion driver (4+ stars prominently marketed). AEP urgency |
| Agentic/AI | 2 | Limited. CenterWell clinics use AI for care coordination. Agent-assisted model for seniors |
| Entitlement | 3 | Medicare Advantage plan types (HMO, PPO, SNP). OTC allowance, SilverSneakers, dental/vision/hearing |

**Key insight:** Medicare-first onboarding is fundamentally different — regulated enrollment periods create urgency-driven conversion, Star Ratings are the primary trust signal, and agent-assisted phone/in-person enrollment dominates for the 65+ demo. QBO equivalent: industry-specific onboarding paths with different trust signals per vertical.

**QBO takeaway:** CMS Star Ratings as conversion driver = QBO should pursue "QBO Verified" certification badge for accountants, creating a trust signal that drives conversion.
