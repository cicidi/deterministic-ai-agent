# Airwallex — Cross-Border Business Banking | **Rating:** 4.0/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Airwallex (Australia-founded, $5.6B valuation, 100K+ businesses) is the **global business account for cross-border companies.** Multi-currency accounts in 60+ currencies. Global Accounts (local bank details in 50+ countries — "virtual local bank accounts"). Borderless Cards (multi-currency corporate cards). Onboarding: full KYB — entity docs, UBO, EIN/tax ID, business registration, source of funds. 2-3 days to approval. Airwallex competes with Wise Business, Revolut Business, and traditional bank international accounts.

**Key insight:** "Get a local bank account in 50+ countries without opening 50 bank accounts" — Airwallex solves the hardest cross-border KYB problem. One KYB application → 50 local banking details. QBO: one QBO account → multi-currency, multi-entity accounting.
