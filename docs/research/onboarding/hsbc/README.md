# HSBC — Global Banking Onboarding | **Rating:** 2.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

HSBC (39M+ customers, 62 countries, $3T+ assets) is the **"world's local bank"** — with the most complex multi-jurisdiction onboarding in banking. HSBC Premier ($75K+), HSBC Jade ($1M+), HSBC Private Banking. Cross-border account opening (open in one country, recognized in others). Onboarding: CIP/KYC per jurisdiction + FATCA/CRS + source of wealth + PEP screening. Known for: some of the most stringent KYC in banking (post-2012 money laundering scandal and $1.9B DPA).

**Key insight:** HSBC's post-scandal compliance is the most stringent KYC in banking — proving that enforcement drives onboarding rigor (like Wells Fargo post-2016). The flip side: notoriously slow onboarding (weeks, not minutes). QBO lesson: compliance maturity = higher trust but slower conversion.
