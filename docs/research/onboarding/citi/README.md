# Citibank — Global Traditional Banking Onboarding

> **Industry:** Banking / Global | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
Citibank (4th largest US bank, 90+ countries) has a unique **cross-border relationship recognition model** — a Citigold client in Singapore is recognized as Citigold in London or New York. Three-tier wealth management: Citi Priority ($30K) → Citigold ($200K) → Citigold Private Client ($1M+). Each tier triggers progressively deeper KYC: basic CIP → Enhanced Due Diligence + source of wealth → comprehensive wealth profile + multi-jurisdiction tax compliance. Citi's 13-country consumer banking retreat (2021+) focuses on wealth hubs.

**Key insight:** Tier-based KYC depth — $30K balance = basic CIP. $200K = EDD + source of wealth. $1M = multi-jurisdiction tax + trust/entity review. KYC scales with relationship value.

**Rating:** 3.5 — best global KYC adaptation among traditional US banks but retreating from consumer markets.
