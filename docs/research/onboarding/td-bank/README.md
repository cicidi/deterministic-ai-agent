# TD Bank — America's Most Convenient Bank Onboarding | **Rating:** 2.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

TD Bank (8th largest US bank by deposits, Canadian parent TD Bank Group) brands itself as **"America's Most Convenient Bank"** — open 7 days, extended hours, drive-through. Consumer: SSN, ID, DOB, address, phone, employment, $0 minimum for TD Simple Checking. Business: EIN, formation docs, UBO, operating agreement. TD Beyond Checking ($25/mo, waived $2,500+). TD Essential Banking (no-overdraft, $4.95/mo). Cross-border advantage: seamless USD/CAD accounts for Canadian-US cross-border customers. TD Ameritrade was Schwab's acquisition.

**Key insight:** 7-day branch hours + extended weekday hours = the most accessible in-person onboarding among major US banks. Branches open when customers can actually visit (evenings, weekends). QBO lesson: support availability as onboarding conversion driver.
