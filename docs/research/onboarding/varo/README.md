# Varo Bank — First All-Digital US Chartered Bank | **Rating:** 3.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Varo (5M+ users) is the **first US neobank to receive a national bank charter** (2020, OCC-approved). Unlike Chime (partner bank model), Varo is a real bank — which changes onboarding: Varo must directly comply with full CIP/BSA/AML as a chartered bank. Onboarding: SSN, ID, DOB, address, phone — entirely digital. Varo Believe (no-credit-check secured card). Varo Advance (up to $500, like Chime SpotMe). Varo to Anyone (P2P). No monthly fees. By being chartered, Varo can hold deposits directly (not through partner banks) and offer products that require a banking license.

**Key insight:** Varo's bank charter means they must do FULL KYC with ZERO branches — proving that 100% digital KYC at chartered-bank compliance standards is possible. QBO: full accounting compliance with zero accountant assistance.
