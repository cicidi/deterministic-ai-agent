# Xero — Cloud Accounting Competitor Onboarding | **Rating:** 3.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Xero (New Zealand, 3.7M+ subscribers, QBO's #1 competitor globally) built **beautiful cloud accounting** with strong bank feed integration (direct bank feeds in UK/AU/NZ — regulatory environment allows this, unlike US). Onboarding: business name, type, industry, address, tax registration (GST/VAT number per country), bank connection (direct feeds or Yodlee), chart of accounts template by industry, opening balances, invoice customization, add team members + accountant access. Xero HQ for accountants. Multi-currency (160+ currencies). Payroll (Xero Payroll/ partner). 800+ app marketplace.

**Key insight:** Direct bank feeds (no Plaid intermediary needed in UK/AU/NZ) make Xero's bank connection step faster than QBO in those markets. Xero's industry-specific chart of accounts templates reduce setup time. QBO should match: pre-built industry templates.
