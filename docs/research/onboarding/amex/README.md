# American Express — Premium Card Onboarding | **Rating:** 3.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Amex (140M+ cards globally) is the **closed-loop network** — issuer + acquirer + network. Charge cards (no preset spending limit) and credit cards with Membership Rewards. Onboarding: SSN, income, employment — no physical ID for many cards. Instant card number on approval (before physical card arrives). Amex Offers, Amex Travel, Centurion Lounges, Platinum Concierge. Business: Amex Business Gold/Platinum, Corporate Green/Gold. Kabbage acquisition (2020) for SMB lending.

**Key insight:** Instant digital card number (usable immediately via Apple Pay) eliminates the "wait for physical card" friction. Amex's "no preset spending limit" on charge cards inverts the traditional credit limit model — spend dynamically approved per transaction. QBO: instant service activation before full verification.
