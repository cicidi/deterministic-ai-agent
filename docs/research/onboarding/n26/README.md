# N26 — European Mobile-First Neobank Onboarding
> **Industry:** Fintech / Neobank (EU) | **Rating:** 4.2/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

## Executive Summary
N26 (8M+ users, Germany-based, operates in 24 EU markets + US) pioneered **8-minute video KYC** — the fastest bank account opening in Europe. German BaFin-regulated with full banking license. Tiers: Standard (free), Smart (€4.90/mo), You (€9.90/mo), Metal (€16.90/mo). N26 Spaces (sub-accounts), N26 Crypto, N26 Insurance. 2021 BaFin restrictions (50K new customers/month cap) forced internal compliance overhaul.

| Dimension | Rating | Key Pattern |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Field Minimization | 5 | 8-minute video KYC. Passport/ID scan + live video call + address verification. No branch, no paper |
| Risk Intelligence | 4 | BaFin-regulated bank. Video KYC + IDnow/WebID + liveness detection. Post-2021 compliance overhaul |
| Conversion | 5 | 8-minute bank account = fastest in Europe. Mobile-only. Instant virtual card. €0 entry |
| Agentic/AI | 3 | N26 chatbot, Spaces automated savings, Insights spending analytics. Perks cashback program |
| Entitlement | 4 | Free → Smart → You → Metal tier ladder. Spaces sub-accounts. N26 Crypto, Insurance |

**Key insight:** N26 proved that full KYC can happen in 8 minutes via video call — displacing the PostIdent/branch model that dominated German banking for decades. Regulatory backlash (BaFin cap) shows the tension between speed and compliance.
