## Executive Summary — Why Coinbase is #7

Coinbase's **"Learn to Earn"** is the most innovative onboarding hook in fintech. Here's how it works concretely:

**The hook:** A prospective user downloads the Coinbase app. They watch a 2-minute educational video about Bitcoin. They answer a single quiz question. Coinbase deposits $3 of BTC into their account. They watch another video about Ethereum. Answer a question. $3 of ETH deposited. The user now has $6-15 of crypto — in an account they can't touch.

**The genius:** The crypto sits there. Untouchable. The user sees it every time they open the app. To withdraw, trade, or send it — they must complete full KYC (photo ID + selfie + SSN). But now they have SKIN IN THE GAME. They voluntarily complete KYC not because Coinbase demanded it, but because they want to access THEIR crypto.

**QBO equivalent:** User signs up with just email. Creates 1 free invoice template. Generates 1 free P&L report. Sees 10 auto-categorized transactions from a connected bank. These "assets" sit in their QBO account — templates, reports, data. To customize them, export them, or access advanced features → complete business verification. User now has skin in the game and WANTS to complete KYC.

**Concrete numbers:** 108M+ verified users. ~200+ crypto assets available. $100M NYDFS fine (2023) for AML/KYC failures — then $50M compliance investment to fix them. Public company (COIN). The most regulated crypto exchange.
