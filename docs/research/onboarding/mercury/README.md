# Mercury — Startup Banking Onboarding | **Rating:** 4.5/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Mercury (100K+ startups, $10B+ deposits) is the **standard US startup bank** — built for tech companies that Y Combinator/VC-backed startups use. Zero-fee business checking, savings, Mercury Treasury, venture debt, SAFE management. Onboarding: EIN, Secretary of State lookup, formation docs, company website, company description (what do you build?), team bios, investor deck (optional). NO personal SSN for founders. Partner banks: Choice Financial Group, Evolve Bank & Trust. Mercury's IO (issuer-processor) card product. API-first.

**Key insight:** Mercury's onboarding reads like a YC application — "what do you build?" not "what's your revenue?" Underwriting based on company viability signals (investor backing, team, product), not credit score. This is the most startup-native financial onboarding in existence.

**Rating:** 4.5 | **QBO takeaway:** Startup-native onboarding — evaluate based on company potential signals, not traditional credit/financial data.
