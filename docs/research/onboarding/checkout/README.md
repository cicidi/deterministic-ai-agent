# Checkout.com — Enterprise Payment Gateway | **Rating:** 3.8/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Checkout.com (UK-based, $40B valuation at peak, processes ~$200B+) is the **enterprise PSP for global merchants** — Netflix, Pizza Hut, Sony, Shein, Binance. Unlike Stripe (self-serve), Checkout.com is high-touch enterprise. Onboarding: full KYB with entity docs, UBO, source of wealth, PCI DSS, compliance review. Known for serving crypto exchanges and high-risk merchants where Stripe hesitates. Card acquiring in 150+ currencies. Fraud detection (Radar competitor). Payouts in local currencies.

**Key insight:** Checkout.com fills the "enterprise + high-risk" gap that Stripe avoids — crypto exchanges, gaming, marketplaces. Their compliance review is more rigorous but allows more business models. QBO: "enterprise onboarding path" for complex multi-entity, multi-currency businesses that self-serve can't handle.
