# Aspiration — Sustainable/Green Banking | **Rating:** 3.3/5 | **Date:** 2026-08-05

Aspiration markets **"sustainable, fossil-fuel-free" banking** — the only climate-conscious positioning studied. Spend & Save accounts ($0-7.99/mo "pay what is fair"), Planet Protection (carbon offset for gas purchases), Conscience Coalition (cashback at mission-driven brands), Aspiration Zero credit card (tree planted per transaction). Onboarding: SSN, ID, DOB, address, phone — standard KYC. Aspiration Redwood Fund (ESG investing). Aspiration Impact Measurement (personal sustainability score).

**Key insight:** Aspiration's "pay what is fair" pricing + mission-driven positioning appeals to a specific psychographic. Green banking as an onboarding differentiator — users choose Aspiration OVER Chime/Ally because of mission alignment. QBO: mission-driven accounting for B Corps and social enterprises.
